# Privacy policy

Effective November 15, 2026. Publisher: [Pankaj Vaghela](https://github.com/pankajvaghela).

Design System Workflow is a downloadable collection of agent instructions and reference files. It contains no hosted backend, account system, analytics code, tracking pixels or bundled editor connector. Installing or using these files does not itself send design documents or conversations to the publisher.

## Information handled

The agent host may read the task description, supplied designs, component details and files needed for your requested work. These may contain personal information that you choose to provide. Processing by your AI provider and any editor or connector you authorize is governed by their policies and settings. This plugin does not change their retention or training controls, and does not make a local-only processing guarantee.

The publisher does not operate storage for your prompts, design files or agent conversations through this package. Consequently there is no publisher-side retention period for those materials. Your host, connected tools and local installation may retain their own copies under their respective controls.

## Public support and distribution

If you voluntarily open a GitHub issue, your GitHub identity and submitted text or attachments are visible to the publisher and other people who can access the public issue. They are used to understand and respond to the report. GitHub hosts this information under its own policies. Public issue history can remain after closure; do not include private designs, credentials or sensitive personal information. You can edit your comments and use GitHub's removal or privacy-request mechanisms. The publisher cannot remove copies others have made.

GitHub and optional installers such as the Skills CLI are separate services. They may process connection or installation information under their own policies. The package does not include their telemetry; installer telemetry controls belong to the installer.

## Your controls and questions

Choose which files and tools your host can access. Stop the workflow, revoke connector access, or remove the installed skills using your host's controls. Removing the files does not delete your provider's conversation history or documents in connected services.

For questions about this package, see [support](SUPPORT.md). Use GitHub's own private privacy channels for sensitive requests about GitHub-hosted data rather than posting that data publicly. Changes to this policy will update this file and its effective date.
